Controls verified

Canonical assets enforced

Only VERSE on Ethereum, fxVERSE on Polygon, and canonical USDC on Ethereum, Polygon and Base are accepted. Clients cannot submit token contracts.

Invoice-level transfer accounting

Invoices anchor to a start block. Finalized ERC-20 Transfer logs identify the payer, transaction, amount and mining time with deduplication.

Wrong and late payments classified

Exact, partial, excess, expired, late and multi-sender transfers are separated. Refund destinations are locked to the observed sender and require merchant authorization.

Non-custodial recovery verified

The merchant receives recovery material once; LiquidFlow does not retain the spend key. Recovery tooling validates canonical contracts and required gas.

Unsafe pooled settlement disabled

The shared-balance ERC-20 route in PaymentGate always reverts. Checkout uses an invoice-specific generated address.

Atomic confirmation and signed webhooks

A distributed lock prevents duplicate confirmation transitions. Webhooks use HMAC signatures and unique delivery identifiers.

External gates before unrestricted mainnet

Independent security review required

A qualified third party must assess the stealth cryptography, contracts and operational key model. Internal tests cannot certify them.

Small-value mainnet rehearsal required

Record all five asset/network routes and exact, partial, excess, recovery and refund cases using controlled funds.

Automated fund movement remains restricted

Gas sponsorship, signed refunds, automated settlement retries and VERSE DEX routing remain disabled or merchant-controlled until separately approved.

Integration rule

Verify webhook signatures, deduplicate LF-Webhook-Id, and call GET /api/payments/:id before releasing valuable goods. See the current API reference.
